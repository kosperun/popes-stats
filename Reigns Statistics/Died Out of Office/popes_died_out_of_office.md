# Popes that died out of office

> **Note:** This list includes popes who left office through resignation, abdication, or deposition, and subsequently died outside of the papal office. Also included is John XXIII (Pisan line), who was considered a legitimate pope until 1958 when he was retroactively declared an antipope by the Catholic Church.

| #   | Name           | Left Office | How            | Death Date         | Age at Death | Location of Death                  | Cause / Circumstances                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --- | -------------- | ----------- | -------------- | ------------------ | ------------ | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Pontian        | 0235-09-28  | Resigned       | 0235-10 (approx.)  | ~60 years    | Sardinia (in exile)                | Exiled to Sardinian mines by Emperor Maximinus Thrax; resigned to allow a successor to be elected. Died shortly after arrival. Venerated as a martyr.                                      |
| 2   | Eusebius       | 0310-08-17  | Deposed        | 0310-10-21         | ~55 years    | Sicily (in exile)                  | Exiled to Sicily by Emperor Maxentius following violent conflict with the Heraclian faction over readmission of apostates. Died within weeks of exile.                                     |
| 3   | Silverius      | 0537-03-11  | Deposed        | 0537-12-02         | ~57 years    | Palmarola island (in exile)        | Deposed at the behest of Empress Theodora and her agent Vigilius; exiled to the Pontine island of Palmarola, where he died of starvation. Venerated as a martyr; patron saint of Ponza.   |
| 4   | Romanus        | 0897-11-01  | Deposed        | Unknown            | Unknown      | Unknown                            | Deposed and confined to a monastery; ultimate fate unknown.                                                                                                                                |
| 5   | Leo V          | 0903-09     | Deposed        | 0903 (approx.)     | ~58 years    | Rome (in prison)                   | Overthrown by Antipope Christopher and imprisoned. Reportedly murdered — either by Christopher or by his successor Sergius III, according to Auxilius of Naples.                           |
| 6   | Benedict V     | 0964-06-23  | Deposed        | 0965-07-04         | ~50 years    | Hamburg (in exile)                 | Deposed by Emperor Otto I in favour of Leo VIII; exiled to Hamburg under the custody of the Archbishop. Died a year later.                                                                 |
| 7   | John XVIII     | 1009-07-18  | Resigned       | 1009-07 (approx.)  | ~44 years    | Rome                               | Voluntarily retired to the monastery of San Paolo fuori le Mura, where he died shortly after. Circumstances of resignation remain historically obscure.                                    |
| 8   | Gregory VI     | 1046-12-20  | Deposed        | 1047-11 (approx.)  | ~47 years    | Cologne (in exile)                 | Deposed at the Council of Sutri by Emperor Henry III for simony; exiled to Cologne. His chaplain during exile was the future Pope Gregory VII. Died c. late 1047.                         |
| 9   | Benedict IX    | 1048-07-17  | Deposed        | c. 1055–1056       | ~43 years    | Grottaferrata, Italy               | Held the papacy three separate times (1032–44, 1045, 1047–48); deposed and excommunicated after his third term. Died as a penitent at the Abbey of Grottaferrata.                         |
| 10  | Celestine V    | 1294-12-13  | Resigned       | 1296-05-19         | ~80–86 years | Fumone castle, Lazio               | Resigned after only 5 months; imprisoned by his successor Boniface VIII at Fumone castle to prevent use as a rival claimant. Rumours of murder persist but are unconfirmed. Canonised 1313. |
| 11  | John XXIII †   | 1415-05-29  | Deposed        | 1419-12-22         | ~49–59 years | Florence                           | Born c. 1360–1370 in Procida. Deposed by the Council of Constance (1415); imprisoned until 1419, then submitted to Martin V, who made him Cardinal of Frascati. Died months later. His tomb by Donatello and Michelozzo stands in the Florence Baptistery. Declared antipope 1958. |
| 12  | Gregory XII    | 1415-07-04  | Abdicated      | 1417-10-18         | ~89–90 years | Recanati, Italy                    | Abdicated to help end the Western Schism; rewarded with the title of Cardinal-Bishop of Porto and Papal Legate of the Marche, a position he held until his death.                         |
| 13  | Benedict XVI   | 2013-02-28  | Abdicated      | 2022-12-31         | 95 years     | Mater Ecclesiae Monastery, Vatican | First pope to abdicate on his own initiative since Celestine V (1294). Remained in the Vatican as Pope Emeritus. Longest-lived pope on record.                                             |

† Considered a legitimate pope until retroactively declared an antipope by the Catholic Church in 1958.
