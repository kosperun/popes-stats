## Age gaps between successive popes
[**Full tables**](https://github.com/kosperun/popes-stats/blob/main/Age%20Statistics/Successor%20Age%20Gaps/successor_age_gaps.md)  

Since 1447, only 13 of 60 popes were older than their predecessor. The last was Benedict XIII in 1724. Every pope since Clement XII (1730) has been younger than the pope before him.

# Conclave Statistics
[**More conclave statistics**](https://github.com/kosperun/popes-stats/blob/main/Conclave%20Statistics/conclaves.md)  

112 papal elections from 1061 to 2025, scraped from Wikipedia. The longest, at Viterbo in 1268–1271, lasted almost three years and took 137 ballots. Since 1831, the median conclave has lasted 3 days.

![conclave_durations](Conclave%20Statistics/conclave_durations.png)

# Cardinal Statistics
[**More cardinal statistics**](https://github.com/kosperun/popes-stats/blob/main/Cardinal%20Statistics/cardinals.md)  

About 4,700 cardinals from Salvador Miranda's *The Cardinals of the Holy Roman Church* and Wikidata, used to reconstruct who elected each pope. In 2005, John Paul II had created 114 of the 117 eligible electors. In 2013 the electorate was split 67 to 50 between Benedict XVI's and John Paul II's cardinals, and Francis had created 80% of the electors who chose Leo XIV in 2025.

![electors_created_by_predecessor](Cardinal%20Statistics/electors_created_by_predecessor.png)

# Family Relationships between Popes
[**Full family research**](https://github.com/kosperun/popes-stats/blob/main/Family%20Statistics/Family%20Relationships%20between%20Popes.md)  

56 of 265 popes had a documented or probable family tie to another pope, and 20 were made cardinals by a papal relative. Innocent X was a great-great-great-grandson of Alexander VI. Paul III's mother was a Caetani, from the family of Boniface VIII.

# General Statistics

## Calendar patterns
[**More calendar statistics**](https://github.com/kosperun/popes-stats/blob/main/General%20Statistics/Calendar%20Patterns/calendar_patterns.md)  

December has the most papal elections. Since 1583, only two popes were elected on a Sunday. Pius XII was elected on his 63rd birthday.

![elections_and_deaths_by_month](General%20Statistics/Calendar%20Patterns/elections_and_deaths_by_month.png)

## Overlapping lifespans
[**More on overlapping lifespans**](https://github.com/kosperun/popes-stats/blob/main/General%20Statistics/Overlapping%20Lifespans/overlapping_lifespans.md)  

14 popes, past, reigning and future, were alive at the same time in 1554–1555. When John XXIII was elected in 1958, every pope who has reigned since was already born.

![popes_alive_at_the_same_time](General%20Statistics/Overlapping%20Lifespans/popes_alive_at_the_same_time.png)
